Joey's James Taylor Tribute Is Sold Out. Here's Where to Catch Him Next.

Joey Interrante's James Taylor tribute with St Pete Collective is sold out. The show runs Friday, July 10 at the Palladium Side Door in St. Petersburg, and every ticket is gone. If you did not grab one, there is good news: Joey has seventeen more live shows around Tampa Bay this July, from Gulfport beach bars to Bayboro blues nights.
