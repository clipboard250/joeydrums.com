How to use it

  1. Follow first. From the St Pete Collective Facebook and Instagram, follow every venue. Like a recent post and leave a short comment when it fits. Check Followed.
  2. Then reach out. Start with the rooms you've already played. Call or email, then check Reached out.

When you pitch: lead with the sold-out shows, and say "the music of James Taylor."
