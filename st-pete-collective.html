St Pete Collective
the music of James Taylor

Seven working musicians from St. Petersburg and Tampa, playing the James Taylor songbook the way it was meant to be heard. Joey plays drums.

See the next show Follow on Facebook

What this band is

The St Pete Collective is a group of musicians who play around Tampa Bay all week in different bands. They came together to do one thing well: play the songs they grew up on, with a full band and real care.

Right now the show is all James Taylor. Two sets, one intermission, the songs you know and a few you may have forgotten you loved. It is a sit-down show, not a bar gig. People come to listen.

The band is based in St. Petersburg, Florida and plays theaters, cultural centers, and listening rooms across Pinellas, Hillsborough, and Sarasota counties.
